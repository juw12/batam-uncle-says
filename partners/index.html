Partner with Batam Uncle Says

A free guide for Singaporeans on a Batam trip: ferry-timed day plans, fair-price checks, hotels, food, spas and oleh-oleh, in English and Chinese.

How it works

  • Your listing stays free. We list places from published guides and our own checks, whether or not they are partners.
  • Partners get a Partner badge, your offer for our users, and a WhatsApp button that opens a chat to your own number. The first line says the guest found you on Batam Uncle Says and gives the code BUS.
  • For guests who arrive with that code, you pay Batam Uncle Says a referral fee you agree to (for example 10% of the bill), settled once a month.
  • No exclusivity, no sign-up fee, stop any time. Order, prices and the fair-price check never depend on payments.

Kerja sama (Bahasa Indonesia)

  • Listing tetap gratis. Kami mencantumkan tempat dari panduan publik dan pengecekan sendiri, partner atau bukan.
  • Partner mendapat lencana Partner, penawaran khusus untuk pengguna kami, dan tombol WhatsApp ke nomor Anda sendiri. Baris pertama pesan menyebut tamu menemukan Anda di Batam Uncle Says dengan kode BUS.
  • Untuk tamu yang datang dengan kode itu, Anda membayar komisi referensi yang disepakati (misalnya 10% dari tagihan), dihitung sebulan sekali.
  • Tanpa eksklusivitas, tanpa biaya daftar, bisa berhenti kapan saja. Urutan, harga, dan cek harga wajar tidak dipengaruhi pembayaran.
