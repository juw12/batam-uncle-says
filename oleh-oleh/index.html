Batam oleh-oleh: what to buy, where, and what Singapore allows

The food gifts people ask for, shops that sell them, and the Singapore Customs and SFA rules for bringing them back. Shops are listed from their own sites and published guides, not yet visited in person.

What to buy

  • Kek pisang (banana cake): The classic Batam gift, in many flavours (Eat within days; traveloka.com)
  • Lapis legit / lapis berendam: Rich layer cake; berendam is the soaked, extra-moist kind (Eat within days; lapisberendam.com)
  • Kerupuk / keripik gonggong: Crackers flavoured with gonggong sea snail; light and keeps well (Keeps well; traveloka.com)
  • Amplang: Crispy fish crackers (Keeps well; acc.co.id)
  • Luti gendang: Fried roll with spiced fish; best eaten soon (Eat soon; traveloka.com)
  • Bolu kemojo: Soft pandan sponge cake from Riau (Eat within days; acc.co.id)

Where to buy

  • Kek Pisang Villa (Imam Bonjol) (Nagoya)
    Batam's best-known banana cake. Other outlets in Batam Centre and at Hang Nadim airport; listed 07:00-22:00 (kekpisangvilla.com)
  • Rumah Kue Wiweka (Nagoya)
    Lapis berendam (soaked layer cake), kue lapis and snacks. Komplek Bumi Indah, below Sari Jaya Hotel; listed 08:00-22:00 (lapisberendam.com)
  • Lapis Berendam (Batam Centre) (Batam Centre)
    Same lapis maker as Rumah Kue Wiweka. Ruko Central Aladin, opposite RS Elisabeth, and Botania (opposite GOR Bandara); listed 07:00-22:00 (lapisberendam.com)
  • Luti Gendang Batam (Batam Centre)
    Luti gendang: fried bread roll with spiced fish, a Riau Islands snack. Outlet in Botania; also sells online (lutigendang.id)
  • Tok Ngah Durian (Batam Centre)
    Durian pancakes and brownies. Ruko KBC Batam Centre; also a counter in Nagoya Hill Mall (idntimes.com)

Bringing it into Singapore

  • Day trip (under 2 days away): no duty-free liquor. Duty-free liquor needs at least 2 days outside Singapore. (customs.gov.sg)
  • Cigarettes and tobacco: no duty-free and no GST relief at all. Declare them. (customs.gov.sg)
  • GST relief on new goods: up to S$100 if away less than 2 days (S$500 if 2 days or more). (customs.gov.sg)
  • Cakes, crackers and other processed food: up to 5 kg in total, for your own use. (sfa.gov.sg)
  • Meat products (anything containing meat, e.g. beef floss or rendang): Indonesia is not an approved source, so leave them. (sfa.gov.sg)

Rules can change. Check the official pages before you buy.
