Money in Batam: exchange rate, cash and paying by QR

Mid-market rate on 6 Nov 2026: S$1 = Rp 14.120 (wise.com). Terminal and airport counters can be 8-12% worse than market (batam.com, Apr 2026).

Tips

  • Use licensed changers only (KUPVA Berizin sign with QR code) (batamgems.com)
  • Best Batam rates are usually around Nagoya Hill; avoid terminal counters (batam.com)
  • SGD 50/100 notes often get a better rate than small notes (batam.com)
  • QRIS: some SG bank apps (e.g. OCBC, UOB) can scan QRIS. Check your app first (batamgems.com)
  • Grab and Gojek both work in Batam. Keep some cash for small stalls (batamgems.com)
