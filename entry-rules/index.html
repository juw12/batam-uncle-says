Singapore to Batam entry rules: passport, arrival cards, duty-free

What Singapore residents need for a Batam day trip, in both directions. Batam (WIB, UTC+7) is 1 hour behind Singapore (UTC+8).

  • Singapore citizens: visa-free for tourism up to 30 days (mfa.gov.sg)
  • Passport valid at least 6 months (mfa.gov.sg)
  • Submit the All Indonesia arrival card online (within 72 h before arrival) (majesticfastferry.com.sg)
  • Coming back: submit the SG Arrival Card (SGAC) within 3 days before arrival (majesticfastferry.com.sg)
  • Away under 48 h: no duty-free alcohol; GST relief only up to SGD 100 (aim-sg.caas.gov.sg)
  • No duty-free allowance for cigarettes / tobacco at any time (aim-sg.caas.gov.sg)
