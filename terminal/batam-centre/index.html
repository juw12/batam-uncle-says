Good to know

  • Grab: Batam Centre terminal to Nagoya: Rp 23.000 – 80.000 (about S$2 – 6) (travjoy.com)
  • Long weekends: Batam Centre terminal queues have hit 2.5 h. Arrive early. (asiaone.com)
  • Terminal and airport counters can be 8-12% worse than market (batam.com, Apr 2026). See money tips.
  • Batam (WIB, UTC+7) is 1 hour behind Singapore (UTC+8).
