Batam day trip from Singapore: ferry, costs, rules and what to do

Ferries leave HarbourFront and Tanah Merah from 07:40 (last at 21:50) and take 45 to 70 minutes. Batam (WIB, UTC+7) is 1 hour behind Singapore (UTC+8).

1. Before you go

  • Singapore citizens: visa-free for tourism up to 30 days
  • Passport valid at least 6 months
  • Submit the All Indonesia arrival card online (within 72 h before arrival)
  • Coming back: submit the SG Arrival Card (SGAC) within 3 days before arrival
  • Away under 48 h: no duty-free alcohol; GST relief only up to SGD 100
  • No duty-free allowance for cigarettes / tobacco at any time

4. Money

  • Use licensed changers only (KUPVA Berizin sign with QR code)
  • Best Batam rates are usually around Nagoya Hill; avoid terminal counters
  • SGD 50/100 notes often get a better rate than small notes

6. Getting home

  • Long weekends: Batam Centre terminal queues have hit 2.5 h. Arrive early.

  • Away under 48 h: no duty-free alcohol; GST relief only up to SGD 100
