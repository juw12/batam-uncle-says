Batam day trip from Singapore: ferry, cost, rules and what to do

HowFerry from HarbourFront or Tanah Merah to one of 6 Batam terminals

How muchS$98 – 164 per person for an example day, ferry from S$76
Watch out
  • Batam (WIB, UTC+7) is 1 hour behind Singapore (UTC+8)
  • Submit the All Indonesia arrival card online (within 72 h before arrival)
  • Coming back: submit the SG Arrival Card (SGAC) within 3 days before arrival
  • Long weekends: Batam Centre terminal queues have hit 2.5 h. Arrive early.

1. Before you go

  • Singapore citizens: visa-free for tourism up to 30 days (mfa.gov.sg)
  • Passport valid at least 6 months (mfa.gov.sg)
  • Submit the All Indonesia arrival card online (within 72 h before arrival) (majesticfastferry.com.sg)
  • Coming back: submit the SG Arrival Card (SGAC) within 3 days before arrival (majesticfastferry.com.sg)
  • Away under 48 h: no duty-free alcohol; GST relief only up to SGD 100 (aim-sg.caas.gov.sg)
  • No duty-free allowance for cigarettes / tobacco at any time (aim-sg.caas.gov.sg)

4. Money

  • Use licensed changers only (KUPVA Berizin sign with QR code) (batamgems.com)
  • Best Batam rates are usually around Nagoya Hill; avoid terminal counters (batam.com)
  • SGD 50/100 notes often get a better rate than small notes (batam.com)
